# PRODUCT.md — Stani i Hoxhës

## Product Purpose
Static marketing website for a seasonal mountain hotel in the Novoseja highlands above Kukës, Albania. Open only in summer (22 May to end of August). No live booking — email-only inquiry form. The site's job is to communicate the retreat's unique character and convert visitors into inquiries.

## Users
- Albanian families and couples seeking a mountain summer escape (primary)
- Albanian diaspora and international travelers (secondary)
- Mobile-first but also desktop research browsing

## Brand Voice
Calm, traditional, unhurried. The language of alpine retreats — not luxury resort branding. Bilingual Albanian (primary) + English. Copy is direct and specific; no travel-brochure fluff. Seasonal awareness is always present.

## Register
brand

## Anti-references
- Generic hotel template sites (uniform cards, stock photos of amenities)
- Resort-chain look (slick, corporate, overly polished)
- Airbnb-style listing layout
- Dark "boutique hotel" aesthetic — this is a sunny alpine retreat
- Chatbot or live booking widget

## Strategic Principles
- Photography is the hero when it arrives. Every placeholder must hold space credibly.
- The bilingual structure is permanent — Albanian is always primary.
- The inquiry form is the only conversion action. Everything leads to it.
- The brand identity (gold + forest green) is non-negotiable. It comes from the logo.
- "Seasonal" is a personality trait, not a disclaimer.
